The Portfolio Gazette

Amazon Nova Hackathon, Agentic AI category. Codename Salzburg. Built solo.

A grounded portfolio-risk copilot for retail investors. It takes your holdings, live quotes, headlines and macro data, then runs an agent investigation that cites its evidence. The risk score comes from a deterministic rules engine; the model never sets it.

plan→fetch→verify→synthesize
